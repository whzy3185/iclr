# ICLR Fit Validation Research Execution Plan

## Objective

Validate whether the current topic can become an ICLR machine learning contribution before large-scale experiments.

Working hypothesis:

> Retrieval context acts as an inference-time inductive bias that shapes the hypothesis distribution of LLMs.

The target is not a general claim about AI and science. The target is an ML mechanism: how evidence exposure changes model-generated hypothesis space.

---

## Stage 1: Problem formulation

Questions:

1. Is the object of study an ML system behavior rather than a sociology phenomenon?
2. Can retrieval be treated as an intervention variable?
3. Can generated research proposals be represented as measurable hypotheses?

Deliverables:

- formal problem definition
- variables
- causal diagram
- falsification criteria

---

## Stage 2: ICLR collision analysis

Investigate:

- retrieval as inductive bias
- RAG behavior changes
- LLM hypothesis generation
- scientific ideation systems
- diversity collapse / mode collapse
- evaluation of LLM reasoning diversity

For every related paper record:

- claim
- mechanism
- experiment
- overlap
- difference

Goal:

Find whether the contribution is genuinely new at the mechanism level.

---

## Stage 3: Paper blueprint generation

Prepare three possible paper forms:

A. Evaluation/measurement paper

B. Mechanism discovery paper

C. Retrieval intervention paper

For each write:

- title
- abstract
- contributions
- required experiments
- reviewer objections

---

## Stage 4: Experiment pre-design

Candidate experiments:

1. Retrieval exposure dose-response

Control shared evidence ratio while keeping relevance distribution fixed.

2. Generative-root comparison

Compare ordinary relevant retrieval with historically validated intellectual roots.

3. Selection pressure analysis

Measure whether ranking systems increase quality while reducing portfolio diversity.

---

## Stage 5: Reviewer simulation

Attack from:

- ML reviewer: Is this machine learning?
- LLM reviewer: Is this obvious?
- Experiment reviewer: Are metrics valid?

Do not proceed to full implementation until major objections have answers.

---

## Decision gate

CONTINUE only if:

- clear ML mechanism exists;
- related work does not already establish the claim;
- experiments can causally test the hypothesis;
- negative results still provide scientific value.

Otherwise kill or reformulate the topic.
