## 2. Executive recommendation

Do not lock the project to the original LLM steering narrative. The most economical candidate alternatives are:

1. Scientific-literature retrieval evaluation: relevance versus independently validated coverage of plausible research approaches.
2. A curated task/resource benchmark: problem-to-evidence relevance judgments, route relations, provenance, temporal/version auditing, and robust splits.
3. A data-centric methods paper: evidence selection or scientific-text relation extraction under noisy labels and provenance constraints.
4. A scientific knowledge-graph paper, if a genuine semantic representation and downstream advantage are developed.
5. The existing controlled LLM behavior paper, retained as an alternative rather than the only possible output.

Human-AI interfaces, social-computing studies, autonomous-agent methods, and causal/uncertainty methods are possible but require substantially different evidence. They should not be adopted merely by changing vocabulary.

New priority venue families: **CIKM, DASFAA, ECML-PKDD, WISE, ISWC**. WSDM, ICDM and SDM are additional strong topic candidates. The earlier COLING/NAACL/EMNLP and ECAI options remain relevant. This is an academic-fit recommendation, not a guarantee of acceptance or fee compliance.

## 6. Six proposed paper forms and minimal new evidence

### A. Retrieval evaluation: which research approaches does search expose?

Working title: **Beyond Topical Relevance: Evaluating Research-Approach Coverage in Scientific Literature Retrieval**.

Question: among documents independently judged relevant to a source-grounded research query, do retrieval methods differ in the validated range of approaches they expose?

Reuse: abstracts, seed construction, provisional source annotations, query/document identity and matching logs.
Add: reviewed query neutrality and query-document relevance; multi-label approach annotations with source spans; held-out queries; BM25, dense retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, a standard diversification baseline, and any proposed method. Freeze comparable candidate budgets and report relevance, supported-route coverage, redundancy, cost, label uncertainty and confidence intervals.

Do not infer diversity from word differences alone. Do not define every route as equally valuable. Human-validated coverage is an instrument, not proof that a returned paper will inspire a discovery. This route does not require thousands of generated proposals and can survive an eventual null LLM-steering result.

Fit: CIKM/WSDM/WISE; DASFAA with sufficient retrieval/data-management contribution. Specific novelty versus scholarly-IR/diversification literature remains to be established.

### B. Curated scientific task/resource benchmark

Working title: **A Provenance-Aware Benchmark for Multi-Approach Scientific Evidence Retrieval**.

Deliver queries, relevance judgments, reviewed problem-method/finding relations, alternative-approach evidence sets, version/date information, split policies and baseline runs. Raw ICLR abstracts remain attributed upstream material; the contribution must be the new task, judgments and evaluation, not recrawling public text.

CIKM's 2026 resource rubric explicitly requires novelty, availability, utility and impact. Its resource track is four pages, not automatically a full research paper for institutional recognition. A resource can also support a full empirical research paper if it yields sufficiently important, validated findings. [S3]

Fit: CIKM resource or full research, ISWC resource if genuinely semantic, COLING for a substantial scientific NLP task. No blanket acceptance/recognition promise.

### C. Data-quality and temporal-validity study

Working title: **When Scientific Retrieval Benchmarks Leak: Auditing Query Construction and Publication-Time Assumptions**.

Question: how do solution-revealing query construction, incorrect date assumptions, identity mismatches and incomplete source coverage alter retrieval evaluations?

Compare explicitly documented source-only protocols, adjudicate false positives in leakage flags, assess ranking/coverage changes, and validate on an independent corpus or venue if making general claims. Do not treat a high automatic flag rate as a measured high defect rate. First-known public dates do not prove absence of earlier versions or model training contamination. No claim of leakage is established by the existing corpus report alone.

Fit: CIKM/DASFAA/WISE; ECML-PKDD if a general correction/learning method is the contribution. Copying one famous audit pattern without a new result is insufficient.

### D. Evidence-selection or learning method

Working title: **Reliable Scientific Evidence Selection under Noisy Approach Labels and Budget Constraints**.

Formalize which observable constraints and utility are being optimized. Demonstrate what existing methods fail to optimize. A new formulation, algorithm, analysis or empirically justified robust estimator is required. Compare exact/heuristic matching, relevance-only and diversification alternatives on effectiveness, computational complexity, sensitivity to label/date error and transfer across datasets.

Do not call standard min-cost maximum-flow code a new algorithm. Do not use a single application dataset to claim universal learning improvements. Data-size replication with duplicates is not an independent dataset.

Fit: DASFAA/ECML-PKDD/ICDM/SDM; EDBT only with substantive query/system scaling.

### E. Existing controlled LLM behavior study

Working title: **Same Question, Different Literature: A Controlled Study of LLM Research Choices**.

Retain certified tasks, frozen randomized packet policies, complete output categories, content/presentation controls, independent-seed inference and actual model outputs. This is a viable alternative, but not the only direction that can reuse the data.

Fit: COLING/NAACL/EMNLP/ECAI. No requirement that every resource/retrieval paper also execute this entire generation program. Baseline dependence and natural-RAG prediction are optional claim-dependent extensions, not results already in hand.

### F. Semantic resource, interactive tool or community study

These are different projects, not cosmetic title variations:

- ISWC: a problem/method/finding/limitation/provenance graph with evaluated relations and useful semantic queries; an unvalidated LLM-to-graph dump is not enough.
- IUI: an interactive tool comparing research approaches, evaluated against a normal search/list interface. Claims about human research behavior require actual human participants and appropriate ethics review; model role-play is not a substitute.
- ICWSM: online review/discussion/citation or collaboration traces with a social-scientific question. Accepted-paper abstracts alone do not identify acceptance causes or causal AI effects.
- AAMAS: agents making sequential evidence-acquisition/planning decisions. A batch proposal-generation prompt is not automatically an autonomous-agent contribution.

## 7. Experimental burden and cost implications

Relative planning assessment, not a measured budget:

- Retrieval evaluation/resource work can avoid most expensive proposal generation. Its main burden is trustworthy source/task judgments and baseline retrieval runs.
- Learning/selection methods add implementation, ablations and generalization datasets. Compute depends on whether training is needed.
- Semantic-graph work adds relation annotation, extraction and structured-query evaluation.
- Controlled LLM behavior adds multi-condition generation and outcome annotation.
- Human-AI/community routes add recruitment/ethics or social-trace acquisition and validity work, which can outweigh GPU cost.

Not all directions need the original 36-seed x 2-model x 5-composition x repeated-generation program. The evidence required is determined by the claim, not inherited from the original ICLR plan.

## 8. Publication charges, registration and track recognition

The user's zero-APC preference remains a separate filter from topic fit.

Springer states that conventional proceedings publication has no publication fee except open access. This is publisher guidance, not a promise of zero conference registration. [S24]

ACM venues such as CIKM/WSDM/RecSys/IUI must be treated as **fee-conditional options**. Institutional ACM Open coverage or a valid waiver must be established before claiming no author APC. A current official 2027 ACM-conference policy lists subsidized APCs of USD500 for members and USD750 for non-members; some other conference pages still repeat 2026 figures, so verify the actual publication-year policy and author eligibility. [S25,S16]

Registration and presentation are separate. WSDM 2027 explicitly requires a registered in-person presenter for accepted main/Findings/short papers. WISE 2026 also requires an author registration fee. No 2027 registration-price guarantee is made for the proposed targets. [S12,S8]

If zero APC is non-negotiable and ACM Open/waiver is unavailable, prioritize further fee verification for non-ACM routes such as DASFAA, ECML-PKDD, WISE and the earlier NLP/general-AI options. Do not quietly drop the fee constraint to make the shortlist longer.

## 9. Recommended project strategy

First develop a common, reusable evidence layer: semantic retrieval scores, independently audited query-document judgments, multi-label scientific-approach relations, date/provenance records and leak-resistant splits. This supports several possible paper types without promising several publications.

Then compare two candidate manuscripts rather than developing all directions at once:

- Track A: scholarly retrieval/evaluation, with CIKM/WISE and topic-appropriate DASFAA as target families.
- Track B: data/learning/selection method, with DASFAA/ECML-PKDD and later ICDM/SDM as target families.

Keep the existing LLM-behavior manuscript available if controlled model experiments produce meaningful evidence. Do not retell the same tables as multiple papers. Distinct papers require materially different questions/contributions and evidence, with disclosure and cross-citation of reused assets. Concurrent submission of substantially overlapping work is not permitted by the consulted CFPs. [S2,S8,S12]

No current result is relabeled as a new scientific discovery. The decision to pivot a scientific experiment requires an explicit task specification, not this venue catalog.
